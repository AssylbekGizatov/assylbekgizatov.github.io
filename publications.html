    Assylbek Gizatov.

Introduction

In a letter to Maurice Solovine, Albert Einstein conceptualized scientific discovery as a cyclical process: an intuitive movement from sensory experience to axioms, followed by logical deduction. In the current technological era, generative AI - and Large Language Models (LLMs) in particular, has mastered induction, statistical pattern recognition over large volumes of data, and is now progressing toward deduction, the form of reasoning responsible for constructing formal proofs. Yet it still lacks the mechanism of abduction: the very root of novel explanatory hypotheses.
