About

I’m a Senior Researcher and Lecturer at the Institute of Physics, Mathematics and Applied Technologies, Kh. Dosmukhamedov Atyrau University. My research focuses on applied machine learning for real-world prediction and decision support.

Alongside my research, I work as an AI Technologies & Enablement Expert at NCOC N.V., focusing on AI and Microsoft Copilot. I help companies adopt AI, enable teams to use it effectively, and evaluate the performance of AI services through analytics. Previously, I developed AI models and generative AI solutions for startups and national companies, including work at Samruk-Kazyna.

I completed a research internship at Chongqing University in collaboration with DJI. I also teach AI and data science as a part-time lecturer in Astana Hub’s Tech Orda program.

I work on urban mobility, labour-market forecasting and digital systems, connecting predictive models with practical applications.

Interests: applied machine learning, predictive modelling, intelligent systems, urban mobility and labour-market analytics.

A Benchmark for Novelty Loss and Machine Abduction in Human–LLM Co-Authored Research Writing

Assylbek Gizatov · Independent research · Work in progress.

Introduction

In a letter to Maurice Solovine, Albert Einstein conceptualized scientific discovery as a cyclical process: an intuitive movement from sensory experience to axioms, followed by logical deduction. In the current technological era, generative AI - and Large Language Models (LLMs) in particular, has mastered induction, statistical pattern recognition over large volumes of data, and is now progressing toward deduction, the form of reasoning responsible for constructing formal proofs. Yet it still lacks the mechanism of abduction: the very root of novel explanatory hypotheses.
