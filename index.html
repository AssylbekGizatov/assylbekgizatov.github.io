About

I’m a Senior Researcher at the Institute of Physics, Mathematics and Applied Technologies, Kh. Dosmukhamedov Atyrau University. My research focuses on applied machine learning for real-world prediction and decision support.

Alongside my research, I work at NCOC N.V., focusing on AI and Microsoft Copilot. I help companies adopt AI, enable teams to use it effectively, and evaluate the performance of AI services through analytics. My previous work includes generative AI.

I work on urban mobility, labour-market forecasting and digital systems, connecting predictive models with practical applications.

I hold a B.Sc. in Mathematical and Computational Sciences from Astana IT University (2022–2025).

Interests: applied machine learning, predictive modelling, intelligent systems, urban mobility and labour-market analytics.
